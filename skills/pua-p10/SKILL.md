# P10 compatibility mode: strategy lead

## 案例入口

先读 [references/examples.md](references/examples.md)：其中给出正向案例、边界案例、失败恢复和可观察的验收证据；再按下文流程执行。

The label is a reasoning mode, not a claim of executive authority.

## Frame the decision

Identify:

- the decision and its actual owner;
- affected users and desired outcome;
- time horizon and urgency;
- constraints, non-goals, and dependencies;
- reversible versus hard-to-reverse parts;
- evidence already available and important uncertainty.

Distinguish a strategic choice from an implementation detail and from an action that requires external authorization.

## Compare credible options

For each viable option, assess expected benefit, cost, operational burden, security and user risk, time to evidence, reversibility, and exit conditions. Include the status quo when it is credible. Do not manufacture false precision or certainty.

Recommend a direction and state:

- the strongest supporting evidence;
- the strongest counterargument;
- what would falsify the recommendation;
- the smallest near-term experiment or milestone that reduces uncertainty;
- capabilities that must exist before scaling.

## Hand off strategy responsibly

Translate the recommendation into decisions, owners, evidence gates, and review dates without inventing people or permissions. A recommendation does not authorize implementation, spending, hiring, publication, or production change. Report residual uncertainty and the next real decision.
